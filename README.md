# Beach Body Gym & Swim Plan

## Goals
- Build shoulders, glutes and abs
- Maintain and develop legs and arms
- Improve range of movement and work towards box splits
- Support swimming and freediving practice
- Keep gym sessions efficient and generally around 45–70 minutes

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | Gym A |
| Tuesday | Swim and mobility |
| Wednesday | Gym B |
| Friday | Gym C |
| Weekend | Optional easy swim and mobility |

If only three sessions fit in a week, prioritise the three gym sessions and add short mobility work at home. If swimming is the higher priority that week, replace Gym C with the pool rather than trying to fit everything in.

## Gym A: Squat, Pull and Shoulders
1. Back squat: 3 sets of 5–8 reps
2. Pull-ups or assisted pull-ups: 3 sets of 6–10 reps
3. Dumbbell shoulder press: 3 sets of 8–12 reps
4. Bulgarian split squat: 3 sets of 8 reps per leg
5. Hanging leg raise: 3 sets of 8–15 reps
6. Cossack squat: 2 sets of 8–10 reps per side

## Gym B: Hinge, Push and Shoulders
1. Romanian deadlift: 3 sets of 6–10 reps
2. Incline dumbbell press: 3 sets of 8–12 reps
3. Chest-supported row: 3 sets of 8–12 reps
4. Cable lateral raise: 3 sets of 12–20 reps
5. Ab-wheel rollout: 3 sets of 6–12 reps
6. Controlled Cossack squat: 2 sets of 8 reps per side

## Gym C: Glutes, Back and Core
1. Hip thrust: 3–4 sets of 8–12 reps
2. Lat pulldown: 3 sets of 8–12 reps
3. Dips or machine chest press: 3 sets of 6–12 reps
4. Lateral raise: 3 sets of 12–20 reps
5. Rear-delt fly: 3 sets of 12–20 reps
6. Core circuit, 2–3 rounds:
   - Plank: 30–60 seconds
   - Side plank: 20–45 seconds per side
   - Dead bug: 8–12 reps per side

## Time-Saving Rules
- Pair non-competing exercises as supersets, such as shoulder press with pull-ups or lateral raises with core work.
- Rest roughly 2–3 minutes after demanding compound sets and 60–90 seconds after smaller exercises.
- Use controlled, comfortable range of movement. Do not sacrifice position to add weight.
- If short on time, complete the first four exercises. Treat core and mobility as short home sessions if necessary.

## Box-Split Mobility: 5–10 Minutes
Complete after training or separately when warm:
1. Cossack squat or side-lunge hold
2. Frog stretch
3. Seated straddle or pancake stretch
4. Gentle supported middle-split hold

Use mild-to-moderate tension, move slowly and avoid forcing painful positions. Progress comes from frequent, controlled practice rather than maximal stretching.

## Swimming and Freediving Support
- Use swimming for technique, general conditioning and recovery.
- Keep hard swim sessions away from the most demanding leg session where practical.
- Do not practise breath-hold exercises alone or perform unsupervised underwater breath holds. Use appropriately qualified supervision and follow the leisure centre's rules.

# Progress Tracking

## Log Every Working Set
Record:
- Exercise
- Weight or assistance used
- Repetitions completed for each set
- Effort using **reps in reserve (RIR)**
- Any pain, technique issue or reduced range of movement

Example:

| Date | Exercise | Load | Sets and reps | RIR | Note |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| 28 Sep | Romanian deadlift | 50 kg | 10, 10, 9 | 2 | Controlled full range |

## Progression Method
Use **double progression**:
1. Keep the same weight until all sets reach the top of the target rep range with good form.
2. Finish most sets with roughly 1–3 good repetitions still possible.
3. Once the top of the range is achieved, increase the load by the smallest practical amount.
4. Return towards the lower end of the rep range and build up again.

Example: for 3 sets of 8–12, progress from 10/9/8 to 12/12/12 before adding weight.

## Monthly Checks
Every four weeks, under similar conditions, record:
- Front, side and back progress photos
- Waist measurement
- Hip or glute measurement
- Body weight, using a weekly average rather than reacting to one reading
- Best controlled set for pull-ups, squat, Romanian deadlift, shoulder press and hip thrust
- Middle-split distance from the floor, measured in the same position
- A simple swim benchmark, such as a repeatable comfortable-distance session or pace, without maximal breath holding

## Review Rules
- Keep the programme unchanged long enough to compare performance consistently.
- If repetitions or load improve while technique and range remain consistent, progress is being made.
- If performance falls for several sessions, reduce a set from the affected exercises or take an easier week.
- Stop and seek appropriate professional advice if an exercise causes sharp, persistent or worsening pain.

## Minimum Effective Week
When work is busy:
- Complete Gym A and Gym B
- Alternate Gym C and a swim session each week
- Do 5 minutes of split mobility on three separate days

Consistency and gradual progression matter more than adding extra exercises.
